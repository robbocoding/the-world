The World Today

Welcome to The World Today.

The world is home to different kinds of creatures, some of which fly, crawl, walk, swim, and many others. Each creature has a special role that keeps our world balanced and beautiful. This website will take you on a journey to discover them.

ABOUT US

This website was created to teach and inspire. We want to help students, teachers, and nature lovers understand how diverse and amazing our world is. From the smallest ant to the largest whale, every creature matters. Our mission is simple: to learn about nature, to protect nature, and to appreciate nature.

OUR CONTENT

We focus on four groups of creatures:

1. Creatures That Fly

Birds, bats, butterflies, and bees. They make our skies beautiful, they pollinate our food crops, and they travel thousands of miles across the world.

2. Creatures That Crawl

Snakes, lizards, snails, spiders, and many insects. They may be small, but they keep the soil healthy and clean up our environment.

3. Creatures That Walk

Lions, elephants, horses, dogs, goats, and humans. They live on land. They show strength, intelligence, and family love.

4. Creatures That Swim

Fish, whales, sharks, dolphins, and turtles. They live in oceans, seas, and rivers. More than 70% of all living things on Earth live in water.
